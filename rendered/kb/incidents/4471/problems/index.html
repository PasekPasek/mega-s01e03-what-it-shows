The 5 shift reports are the ones the desk read as a one-day move. They stay counted under parser, where the desk filed them by tenant, and have their own ticket as well.

The counts come from the export at render time: one report per id, the cause from the tenant’s locale and booking form (causes.ts), and the desk’s own list of one-day moves (desk-notes.json). The intake tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser; it is shown for comparison and decides nothing.

parser: day and month swap

The shared parser reads every date month first. A day-first tenant’s 7 April arrives as 4 July. It is the bug #4471 names, and it covers every channel of every tenant except booking-v3 forms. Fixed: fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales. Ticket closed.

hint: the booking-v3 form tells people to type month first

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. On day-first tenants that run it, people type what the hint says, and the form reads it the tenant’s way. The swap looks the same, but the fix is in the form, not the parser. Won’t fix: the booking-v3 form is retired, so the hint is gone with it. Ticket closed as not planned.

shift: the date moves by one day

These reports are not a swap. Tuesday 12 May shows as Monday 11 May. A client in Lisbon gets Tuesday and one in Toronto gets Monday for the same meeting. An invite arrives before the booking is made. That points at time zone handling (a date stored as a moment, read in another zone), not at day order. The desk spotted these and filed them under parser by tenant. The parser fix does not reach them: a date read in the right order can still land on the wrong day. This ticket stays open.
