#4471 was merged as one issue because the reports share subject lines (decision 0001). Read by what the dates in them show, the reports split into three problems. Each one needs its own fix, so each now has its own ticket.

Fixing the parser will not close #4471. The other two problems keep coming in after that fix ships.

The counts come from the export at render time: one report per id, the cause from the tenant’s locale and booking form (causes.ts), and the desk’s own list of one-day moves (desk-notes.json). The intake tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser; it is shown for comparison and decides nothing.

parser: day and month swap

The shared parser reads every date month first. A day-first tenant’s 7 April arrives as 4 July. It is the bug #4471 names, and it covers every channel of every tenant except booking-v3 forms.

hint: the booking-v3 form tells people to type month first

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. On day-first tenants that run it, people type what the hint says, and the form reads it the tenant’s way. The swap looks the same, but the fix is in the form, not the parser. Fixing the parser alone leaves these reports coming in.

shift: the date moves by one day

These reports are not a swap. Tuesday 12 May shows as Monday 11 May. A client in Lisbon gets Tuesday and one in Toronto gets Monday for the same meeting. An invite arrives before the booking is made. That points at time zone handling (a date stored as a moment, read in another zone), not at day order. The desk spotted these and filed them under parser by tenant. They need their own ticket.

What to change on the desk

  • Do not merge on subject alone. Three bugs share the same subject lines.
  • Count one report per id: the export repeats rows across pages.
